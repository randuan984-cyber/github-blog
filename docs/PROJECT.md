# Personal Engineering & Learning Portfolio

> **Specification Version:** 1.0  
> **Created:** 2026-08-15  
> **Status:** Active Development

---

## Problem

As an engineer in active learning, I need a persistent, verifiable record of my growth journey — not a static resume, but a living archive. Traditional resumes flatten the learning process. LinkedIn rewards social activity over engineering depth. GitHub alone doesn't tell the story of *why* I chose a project, *what went wrong*, or *what I learned*.

This portfolio exists to solve that gap.

---

## Target Users

| User | Primary Goal | Key Questions |
|---|---|---|
| **HR / Recruiter** | Understand who this person is in 30 seconds | Who are they? What do they build? How do they communicate? |
| **Technical Interviewer** | Verify engineering ability, see real problem-solving | Can they articulate trade-offs? Do they understand what they built? |
| **Mentor / Professor** | Understand current level and trajectory | What are they actively learning? What problems are they exploring? |
| **Fellow Developer** | Learn from documented engineering problems | Are their notes useful? Can I relate to their journey? |
| **Myself (Future)** | Review my own growth trajectory | What was I doing in 2026? What decisions did I make and why? |

---

## Goals

1. **Showcase real projects** — with full attribution for open-source origins
2. **Document the learning process** — including failures, dead ends, and pivots
3. **Record engineering problems and solutions** as searchable notes
4. **Maintain a learning timeline** as a chronological record
5. **Support open-ended research exploration** without premature specialization
6. **Be maintainable long-term** — adding content requires only Markdown + git push
7. **Be credible** — every statement must be truthful and verifiable

---

## Non-Goals

| Not This | Reason |
|---|---|
| Social platform / comments | Adds complexity, no value for interviewer audience |
| Complex CMS / admin UI | Markdown + frontmatter is sufficient and faster |
| User authentication | Public read-only site |
| Commercial SaaS features | Out of scope |
| Fabricated project metrics | Violates truthfulness principle |
| Pre-defined research direction | Research direction will emerge from real work |

---

## Success Criteria

- [ ] An HR can understand "who I am / what I build / what I'm learning" in under 30 seconds
- [ ] A technical interviewer can trace from project overview → problem → my implementation → lessons learned
- [ ] Adding a blog post requires only: create markdown file → git push
- [ ] The site builds cleanly on every `git push` to `main`
- [ ] All content is truthful and verifiable

---

## Truthfulness Pledge

> **This site will never contain:**
> - Fabricated work experience or company names
> - Inflated project metrics (stars, users, downloads)
> - Research results that do not exist
> - Projects claimed as original when they are open-source
> - Awards, publications, or recognitions not actually received
>
> **When content does not yet exist, use:** `Coming Soon`, `In Progress`, `Currently Learning`, `Exploring`

---

## Content Philosophy

Engineering is not just about what you built.  
It is about:
- **Why** you chose to build it
- **What problem** you were trying to solve
- **What went wrong** along the way
- **How you investigated** the failure
- **What you learned** from it

This portfolio documents the full arc — not just the polished result.
